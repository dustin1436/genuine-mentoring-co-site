The model

The Jethro and Moses pattern.

Exodus 18 gives a picture of mentorship that is practical, relational, and deeply human: a wiser voice sees what a man is carrying and helps him build a life that can hold it.

This is the spirit of Genuine Mentoring Co. It is not control, pressure, hype, or dependence. It is wisdom alongside responsibility.

Mentorship, not therapy, hype coaching, or a mastermind.

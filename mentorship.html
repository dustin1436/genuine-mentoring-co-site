The rhythm

One-on-one mentorship, simple enough to stay real.

Genuine Mentoring Co. is built around steady 90-minute conversations every three weeks. Enough time to go deep, enough space for life to happen between sessions.

Who it is for

Men navigating the weight of becoming.

Starting

Career launch, first major decisions, early responsibility, and learning how to walk with confidence without pretending.

Responsibility

Marriage, fatherhood, leadership, providing, and the quiet pressure of becoming someone others rely on.

Tribulation

Hardship, loss, confusion, failure, or a season where the old way of carrying things is no longer working.

Provision

Building, stewarding, creating stability, and becoming strong without becoming hard.

How it works

The process is personal, but the rhythm is steady.

Discovery conversation

A short first conversation to understand your season, what you are carrying, and whether this is the right fit.

Set the focus

Name the two or three things that matter most right now. This is not a curriculum. It is a focused relationship.

Meet every three weeks

Sessions are 90 minutes. The cadence gives room for honest conversation and time for the work to settle.

Review the season

Every few sessions, step back together. Is this still the right work? Is the season shifting? Adjust with wisdom.

What it is not

Clear boundaries keep the work honest.

Genuine Mentoring Co. is not therapy, counseling, a mastermind group, motivational hype coaching, or a rigid program. If you need a licensed counselor, Dustin will honor that distinction. If you need a consultant, this is not packaged as that either.

This is mentorship: grounded conversation, wise questions, spiritual perspective, and practical encouragement from a trusted voice.

"The goal is not to make a man dependent on another voice. The goal is to help him hear clearly, walk honestly, and become steady."

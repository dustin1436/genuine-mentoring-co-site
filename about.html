Founder and mentor

A grounded voice for men learning how to carry what matters.

Dustin Herron founded Genuine Mentoring Co. to offer men a steady voice in seasons where responsibility, pressure, faith, and calling begin to converge.

The story

Pastoral wisdom, founder reality, and relational steadiness.

Dustin spent nearly a decade in pastoral ministry at Gateway Church, walking with people through formative seasons of life, faith, pain, growth, and decision. That background still shapes the way he listens, asks questions, and speaks into a man's life.

He is also a husband, father, founder, franchisor, and business owner. He understands what it feels like to carry vision and responsibility at the same time, and he knows the difference between sounding strong and actually becoming anchored.

Genuine Mentoring Co. grew out of that intersection: pastoral wisdom, practical experience, and a desire to help men become steady in the places that matter most.

What Dustin brings

Not a performance. A presence.

The work is steady and personal. It is not about impressing a man with answers. It is about helping him tell the truth, see clearly, and walk with wisdom.

The posture

This is not about being impressive. It is about being genuine.

Some men need counsel from a licensed professional. Some need a business consultant. Some need a peer group. Genuine Mentoring Co. is different.

It is for the man who needs a trusted older voice, a steady place to process, and wise conversation that meets him where he actually is.
